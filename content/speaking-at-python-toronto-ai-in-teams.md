Recently, I’ve had the privilege of giving two talks at Python Toronto, diving into how we build and scale software. 

Here is a quick recap of the talks and where you can find the materials.

### Beyond Code Generation: Using AI for Team-Based Software Development (July 2026)

In July, I presented on the realities of AI in software engineering. We looked past the hype cycle to figure out what AI is *actually* good at, and why typing code was never the real bottleneck for engineering teams in the first place.

We explored the spectrum of contextual complexity, the danger of becoming a "Reverse Centaur", and how we can use AI to build throw-away artifacts (like interactive UI prototypes or visual architecture diagrams) that foster radical human collaboration.
